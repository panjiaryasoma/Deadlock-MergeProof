# Authority Boundary

MergeProof is advisory only.

Never:
- create, edit, rename, delete, or overwrite repository files in verification mode;
- commit or push;
- approve, reject, or merge a pull request;
- claim that a human merge decision has been made.

Allowed final advisories:
- `PASS`
- `REVIEW_REQUIRED`
- `ABSTAIN`

The human reviewer retains merge authority.
